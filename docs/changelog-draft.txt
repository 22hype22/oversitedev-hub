## Oversite Network  ·  Weekly Update  ·  Sep 3 – Sep 7
+ Rewrote every slash command description and option hint so they read like a person wrote them, with no staff tags or brackets
+ Added a Vouches block. Buyers run /vouch to rate a designer 1 to 5 stars with a short review, every vouch posts a card in your vouch channel, /vouches shows a designer's average and recent reviews, and the pricing board shows each designer's star rating next to their name
+ The Leave a Review button on package receipts now leaves a vouch for the designer who posted the package
+ Added a Roblox group ID box under API keys and credentials. The Robux Locker, sales tracking, and Roblox Group Sync all read it, so your group only has to be entered once
+ Staff reminders on claimed orders. If a customer has waited 12 hours with no staff reply, the bot pings whoever claimed it. At 24 hours it pings the ticket's staff roles along with the claimer
+ Queue updates for customers. When an open order moves in the line, the customer gets a message in their ticket saying they moved up to 3rd, were moved back, or are next up
+ When an order closes, the customer now gets a DM with a summary, the transcript, and a button to vouch for the designer who handled it
+ Orders now move through two stages on their own. When the payment comes through the ticket is marked in progress, and when the designer logs the order with /orderlog it is marked completed and the customer is told. /progress sets either by hand
+ Opening an order now asks you to tick a box agreeing to the Sales and Refund Policy, and the agreement is written into the ticket with the time
+ Added /away and /back for designers. Away hides you from the pricing board, tells the customers in your claimed tickets when you're back, and pauses the staff reminders on those tickets until you return
+ Running /payment inside an order ticket now links that payment to the order. When the customer pays, the bot posts in the ticket that the payment came through and counts it toward the designer who claimed it
+ Customers get the vouch DM the moment their order is marked completed, not only when the ticket closes
+ Added /myorders so customers can see their open orders, who has them, and where they are, plus their past orders. Staff can look up any member
+ Added a Sales Stats block. /sales shows each designer's completed orders, packages sold, and Robux and USD revenue for the week, month, or all time, and on the 1st of every month the bot posts last month's recap with the top designer
+ Drop down menu options can use custom emojis. Type :emojiname: at the start of the option and it shows the emoji instead of the text
+ Drop down options that point at a link or a channel now work. Picking one sends you a private message with the link or the channel instead of Discord saying the bot did not respond
